Data Sources

  • OSPI F-196 Financial Reports — Year-end actual expenditures and revenues for Fiscal Year 2023-24 and Fiscal Year 2024-25. Source: ospi.k12.wa.us
  • OSPI F-195 Budget Documents — Adopted budget data for Fiscal Year 2025-26 (district code 17409). Accessed via OSPI SAFS data files.
  • District Budget Publications — Board-adopted budget documents from tahomasd.us.
  • King County Elections — Levy and bond election results and voter records.
  • Ballotpedia — Historical levy/bond election data and ballot measure text.
  • OSPI Report Card — Smarter Balanced Assessment proficiency rates, graduation rates, and enrollment data.
  • Moody's Investors Service — Credit rating (Aa2) and Washington State credit enhancement (Aaa guarantee).
  • U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey — Community demographics (median income, homeownership, population growth).
  • King County Assessor — Assessed valuations and property data for levy rate calculations.
  • OSPI K-12 All Staff Salary Database — Individual salary records for certificated and classified staff.
  • Washington School Employees Benefits Board (SEBB) — Employer and employee benefits rates.
  • Washington Department of Retirement Systems (DRS) — TRS and Public Employees' Retirement System (PERS) pension contribution rates.
  • Niche.com, U.S. News & World Report — District and school rankings.
  • District Capital Facilities Plan — Capital improvement projects and facility planning.

Methodology Notes

  • Fiscal Year 2025-26 General Fund revenue category totals are from the OSPI F-195 adopted budget (district code 17409). Individual line items within each revenue category are proportionally distributed from the F-195 category total using Fiscal Year 2024-25 F-196 actual shares.
  • Revenue and expenditure categorizations follow the Washington State Accounting Manual for Public School Districts (AMPS) chart of accounts.
  • Per-pupil calculations use General Fund expenditures divided by total full-time equivalent (FTE) enrollment.
  • Fund balance ratios follow Government Finance Officers Association best practices: ending fund balance / total expenditures.
  • Year-over-year (YoY) changes compare current year budgeted to prior year OSPI F-196 actuals.
  • Salary estimates derived from OSPI personnel data and comparable King County district salary schedules.
  • Peer district comparisons use same-year OSPI data with enrollment-weighted adjustments.

Standards & Compliance

This dashboard follows transparency standards recommended by the Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) and the Association of School Business Officials (ASBO). Financial data is presented in accordance with Governmental Accounting Standards Board (GASB) standards, with operating (General Fund) and restricted funds displayed separately.
